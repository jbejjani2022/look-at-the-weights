FORBIDDEN: running the language model yourself on any input, even partially. That means no forward pass of any text or token sequence in your code; no feeding token embeddings (or anything computed from them) through the attention or MLP layers with their nonlinearities (activation functions, gates, softmax, normalisation of a hidden state); no logits or probabilities that the model would produce for any text; no text generation; no evaluation of any kind; and no training or optimisation of the model. The one exception is the FORWARD-PASS TOOL below: it runs the two models for you on texts you choose and returns, for each, the most likely next tokens with their probabilities and a greedy continuation; you get no internal activations (no attention weights, hidden states or MLP values).
